Zero to Major The CS2 pro career game

You are 16 and you just hit FACEIT level 10. Climb the Elo ladder, get scouted, sign with real teams and try to lift the Major before you retire. Every choice counts, and so does luck.

How Zero to Major works

  1. Create your player: nickname, nationality, favourite map and one of the five roles (AWPer, entry fragger, lurker, support, in-game leader).
  2. Each season is a series of choices: training, team life, contracts, social media, map vetoes and match situations on the real CS2 maps.
  3. Your choices change eight attributes, your team chemistry, your toxicity, your aura and your popularity. Bad decisions can send you to the bench.
  4. The 32 best teams in the world play the Major: three stages, then quarter-finals, semi-finals and the grand final.
  5. Every year ends with a world Top 20. A career runs from 16 to retirement and ends with a rating out of 100.

Zero to Major FAQ

Is Zero to Major free?

Yes. The game runs in your browser, with no account and no download. Your career is saved on your device.

Are the teams and players real?

The teams and rosters come from Valve's official world ranking, and player roles and nationalities from Liquipedia. Everything that happens in a career is fiction: transfers, results, rankings and events are invented by the game, and the recurring characters (your rival, your agent, your coach) do not exist.

How long does a career take?

About 20 to 25 minutes, from your first Elo climb at 16 to your retirement.

Does my role change the game?

Yes. An AWPer, an entry fragger, a lurker, a support and an in-game leader rely on different attributes and get different situations. Around 30, your team may ask you to become its in-game leader.

Does my nationality matter?

Yes. Teams from your country and your region contact you more easily, like in the real scene. International teams recruit everywhere.
