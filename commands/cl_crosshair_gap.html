cl_crosshair_gap

Crosshair

Space between the center and the crosshair lines, in pixels. Sets the empty space in the middle of the crosshair, in pixels for the screen height set in cl_crosshair_screen_height. A small gap (0 to 2) keeps the lines close to the target's head; a negative value makes the lines overlap the center. On ClutchCrosshair, most pros use a gap of 1 or 2.

cl_crosshair_gap 1

Syntax

cl_crosshair_gap <pixels>

ValueWhat it does
-2Lines overlap the center
0Lines touch the center
2Small gap, very common

Examples

cl_crosshair_gap 1
cl_crosshair_gap -1

How to use cl_crosshair_gap in CS2

  1. Enable the developer console in Settings › Game › Enable Developer Console.
  2. Press ~ to open the console, type the command and press Enter.
  3. To apply it every time you launch CS2, add the line to an autoexec.cfg file in your cfg folder.
