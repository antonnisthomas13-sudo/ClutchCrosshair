What to use instead in CS2

cl_crosshair_outlinethickness no longer exists in CS2. Turn the outline on or off with cl_crosshair_drawoutline, and choose its color and opacity with cl_crosshairoutline_r, _g, _b and _a.

Use cl_crosshair_drawoutline instead: adds an outline around the crosshair.
