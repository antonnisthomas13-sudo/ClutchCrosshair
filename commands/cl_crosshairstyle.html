cl_crosshairstyle

Crosshair

Chooses a static or dynamic crosshair. Picks how the crosshair reacts when you move and shoot. Style 4 (static) never moves and is used by almost every pro. Style 2 keeps a fixed center while the outer parts react to recoil and movement. Style 5 is the legacy CS:GO-style dynamic crosshair.

cl_crosshairstyle 4

Syntax

cl_crosshairstyle <2|4|5> · Default value: 2

ValueWhat it does
2Default: fixed center, outer parts show recoil and spread
4Static: never moves (most pros)
5Legacy: CS:GO-style fake recoil

Examples

cl_crosshairstyle 4

How to use cl_crosshairstyle in CS2

  1. Enable the developer console in Settings › Game › Enable Developer Console.
  2. Press ~ to open the console, type the command and press Enter.
  3. To apply it every time you launch CS2, add the line to an autoexec.cfg file in your cfg folder.
