cl_radar_scale

HUD & radar

Zoom level of the radar. Sets how much of the map the radar shows, from 0.25 to 1. A lower value zooms out and shows more of the map: many players use 0.3 to 0.4 to see every bombsite at once.

cl_radar_scale 0.35

Syntax

cl_radar_scale <0.25-1> · Default value: 0.7

Examples

cl_radar_scale 0.35

How to use cl_radar_scale in CS2

  1. Enable the developer console in Settings › Game › Enable Developer Console.
  2. Press ~ to open the console, type the command and press Enter.
  3. To apply it every time you launch CS2, add the line to an autoexec.cfg file in your cfg folder.

cl_radar_scale FAQ

What does cl_radar_scale do in CS2?

Zoom level of the radar. Sets how much of the map the radar shows, from 0.25 to 1. A lower value zooms out and shows more of the map: many players use 0.3 to 0.4 to see every bombsite at once.

What is the default value of cl_radar_scale?

The default value of cl_radar_scale in CS2 is 0.7.

Does cl_radar_scale need sv_cheats?

No. cl_radar_scale works without sv_cheats, including in official matchmaking.
