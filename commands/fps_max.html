fps_max

Graphics & FPS

Caps your frame rate. Limits how many frames per second CS2 renders. 0 removes the limit. A cap a bit above your monitor's refresh rate (or a stable value your PC can always reach) gives smoother frame times than an unlimited frame rate that keeps jumping.

fps_max 0

Syntax

fps_max <0|number> · Default value: 400

ValueWhat it does
0No limit
400Default cap
300Example of a stable cap

Examples

fps_max 0
fps_max 300

How to use fps_max in CS2

  1. Enable the developer console in Settings › Game › Enable Developer Console.
  2. Press ~ to open the console, type the command and press Enter.
  3. To apply it every time you launch CS2, add the line to an autoexec.cfg file in your cfg folder.

fps_max FAQ

What does fps_max do in CS2?

Caps your frame rate. Limits how many frames per second CS2 renders. 0 removes the limit. A cap a bit above your monitor's refresh rate (or a stable value your PC can always reach) gives smoother frame times than an unlimited frame rate that keeps jumping.

What is the default value of fps_max?

The default value of fps_max in CS2 is 400.

Does fps_max need sv_cheats?

No. fps_max works without sv_cheats, including in official matchmaking.
