sensitivity

Mouse

Your mouse sensitivity. Sets how fast your view turns when you move the mouse. Pros usually multiply it with their mouse DPI to compare: 400 DPI at 2.0 or 800 DPI at 1.0 both give 800 eDPI, the typical range for CS2 pros being about 600 to 1200 eDPI.

sensitivity 1.2

Syntax

sensitivity <value> · Default value: 2.5

Examples

sensitivity 1.2
sensitivity 2

How to use sensitivity in CS2

  1. Enable the developer console in Settings › Game › Enable Developer Console.
  2. Press ~ to open the console, type the command and press Enter.
  3. To apply it every time you launch CS2, add the line to an autoexec.cfg file in your cfg folder.

sensitivity FAQ

What does sensitivity do in CS2?

Your mouse sensitivity. Sets how fast your view turns when you move the mouse. Pros usually multiply it with their mouse DPI to compare: 400 DPI at 2.0 or 800 DPI at 1.0 both give 800 eDPI, the typical range for CS2 pros being about 600 to 1200 eDPI.

What is the default value of sensitivity?

The default value of sensitivity in CS2 is 2.5.

Does sensitivity need sv_cheats?

No. sensitivity works without sv_cheats, including in official matchmaking.
