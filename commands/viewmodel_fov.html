viewmodel_fov

Viewmodel

How far your weapon model is from the camera. Changes the field of view of your weapon model only, from 54 to 68. A higher value pushes the gun further away and shows more of the screen; many players use the maximum, 68.

viewmodel_fov 68

Syntax

viewmodel_fov <54-68> · Default value: 60

Examples

viewmodel_fov 68

How to use viewmodel_fov in CS2

  1. Enable the developer console in Settings › Game › Enable Developer Console.
  2. Press ~ to open the console, type the command and press Enter.
  3. To apply it every time you launch CS2, add the line to an autoexec.cfg file in your cfg folder.
