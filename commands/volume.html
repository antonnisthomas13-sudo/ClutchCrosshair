volume

Audio

Main game volume. Sets the master volume of the game, from 0 to 1. Many players keep it fairly high so they can hear footsteps clearly, and lower music instead.

volume 0.5

Syntax

volume <0-1>

Examples

volume 0.5

How to use volume in CS2

  1. Enable the developer console in Settings › Game › Enable Developer Console.
  2. Press ~ to open the console, type the command and press Enter.
  3. To apply it every time you launch CS2, add the line to an autoexec.cfg file in your cfg folder.
