How to Improve Your Aim in CS2: Routine and Aim Maps

Aim · Updated 19/10/2026 · 2 min read

Aim improves with regular, focused practice, not with endless hours of mindless shooting. This guide gives you a short daily routine, the kinds of workshop maps that help most, and a practice config you can paste in your own server.

A 20-minute daily routine

TimeExerciseGoal
5 minBots on an aim map (Aim Botz type)Warm up: crosshair at head height, smooth flicks
5 minSpray practice against a wall with sv_showimpacts 1Learn the AK-47 and M4 patterns
10 minDeathmatch (official or community)Apply it against real moving players

Short and regular beats long and rare: 20 focused minutes every day helps more than 3 hours once a week.

Aim maps worth trying

Search the CS2 Steam Workshop for these kinds of maps and subscribe to the ones you like:

  • Aim Botz style maps: rows of bots to practice flicks, tracking and headshots.
  • Prefire maps: walk through real map positions and kill bots placed where enemies usually hide.
  • Recoil maps: show spray patterns so you can learn to control them.
  • Movement maps: practice counter-strafing, so you are fully stopped when you shoot.

Your own practice server

Load a map with map de_mirage (or any map), open the console and paste this config. It gives you infinite ammo, lets you buy anywhere, removes the round timer and shows where your bullets land:

sv_cheats 1
bot_kick
mp_warmup_end
mp_freezetime 0
mp_roundtime_defuse 60
mp_ignore_round_win_conditions 1
mp_buy_anywhere 1
mp_buytime 9999
mp_maxmoney 60000
mp_startmoney 60000
sv_infinite_ammo 1
sv_showimpacts 1
mp_restartgame 1

Every command is explained in our CS2 console commands list.

Habits that make the biggest difference

  • Crosshair placement: keep your crosshair at head height and close to where enemies will appear. Good placement means you barely need to flick.
  • Counter-strafing: tap the opposite movement key before shooting so you are fully stopped. Moving shots are inaccurate in CS2.
  • Burst at range: at long distance, shoot short bursts or single taps instead of spraying.
  • Keep the same settings: changing sensitivity or crosshair every week resets your muscle memory. Find a pro crosshair you like on ClutchCrosshair and stick with it.

How to Improve Your Aim in CS2 FAQ

How can I improve my aim in CS2 fast?

Practice 20 focused minutes every day: warm up on an aim map, practice sprays, then play deathmatch. Work on crosshair placement and counter-strafing.

What is the best aim map in CS2?

Aim Botz style maps are the classic warm-up. Add prefire maps to learn real angles and recoil maps to master sprays.

How do I make a practice server in CS2?

Type map followed by a map name (map de_mirage), then paste a practice config: sv_cheats 1, sv_infinite_ammo 1, mp_buy_anywhere 1, mp_roundtime_defuse 60 and mp_restartgame 1.

Does sensitivity affect aim in CS2?

Yes: too high makes small corrections hard, too low makes turning slow. Most pros stay between about 600 and 1200 eDPI.
