Best CS2 Settings (2026): Video, Game and Audio

Settings · Updated 19/10/2026 · 2 min read

Good settings in CS2 do two things: they make enemies easy to see, and they keep your frame rate high and stable. Visual beauty comes last. This guide goes through the video, game and audio options and explains what each one changes, so you can make your own choices instead of copying random numbers.

Video settings

Open Settings › Video › Advanced Video. The table below favors visibility and FPS, the choice of most competitive players:

SettingRecommendedWhy
Boost Player ContrastEnabledMakes player models stand out from the background.
Wait for Vertical SyncDisabledV-Sync adds input delay.
NVIDIA Reflex Low LatencyEnabledLowers input delay on supported graphics cards.
Multisampling Anti-Aliasing2x or 4x MSAASmoother edges; lower it first if you need FPS.
Global Shadow QualityMedium or HighShadows can reveal players around corners.
Model / Texture DetailLow or MediumLittle effect on visibility, frees up FPS.
Shader DetailLowFewer effects, clearer image.
Particle DetailLowLess visual noise from effects.
Ambient OcclusionDisabledCosts FPS without helping you spot enemies.
High Dynamic RangePerformanceFaster, with a very similar image.
FidelityFX Super ResolutionDisabledUpscaling makes the image blurry, bad for spotting far enemies.

Change one setting at a time and check your FPS with cl_hud_telemetry_frametime_show 2. The right settings are the ones that keep your FPS stable on your PC.

Resolution and display mode

Use Fullscreen as display mode for the lowest input delay. For the resolution, there is no single best answer: native 16:9 gives the sharpest image and the widest view, while 4:3 stretched makes models look wider. Our stretched resolution guide explains the trade-offs in detail.

Game and HUD settings

  • Radar: zoom out with cl_radar_scale 0.35 and use cl_radar_always_centered 0 so the radar shows more of the map. Make it bigger with cl_hud_radar_scale.
  • Viewmodel: viewmodel_fov 68 pushes your weapon away and shows more of the screen.
  • HUD color: pick a color you read easily with cl_hud_color.
  • Telemetry: show your FPS and ping in Settings › Game › Telemetry to spot performance problems.
  • Crosshair: start from a pro crosshair or build yours in the crosshair generator.
cl_radar_scale 0.35
cl_radar_always_centered 0
cl_hud_radar_scale 1.15
viewmodel_fov 68

Audio settings

  • Play with headphones: CS2's positional audio is made for them.
  • Try each EQ Profile in the audio settings and keep the one where footsteps sound clearest to you.
  • Keep the main volume fairly high and turn down the music instead (round start, MVP, menu music).
  • Lower the voice chat with snd_voipvolume if your teammates cover the footsteps.
snd_roundstart_volume 0
snd_mvp_volume 0
snd_menumusic_volume 0

Best CS2 Settings FAQ

What are the best CS2 settings for FPS?

Lower Shader, Particle and Model/Texture Detail, disable Ambient Occlusion and FidelityFX Super Resolution, set High Dynamic Range to Performance, and keep V-Sync off. Then check your FPS with the telemetry display.

Should I enable Boost Player Contrast in CS2?

Yes. It makes player models stand out from the background, which helps you spot enemies faster.

Is FidelityFX Super Resolution good for CS2?

Usually not for competitive play: it renders the game at a lower resolution and upscales it, which makes far enemies blurry.

What audio settings do pros use in CS2?

Most play with headphones, keep the main volume fairly high and mute the music (round start, MVP, menu) so footsteps are easier to hear.
