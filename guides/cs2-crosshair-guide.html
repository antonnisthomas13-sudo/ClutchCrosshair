How to import a crosshair code

  1. Copy a code, for example from a pro crosshair page.
  2. In CS2, open Settings › Game › Crosshair.
  3. Click Share or Import, paste the code and confirm.

Since the CS2 update of September 30, 2026, codes start with CS (without dashes). Old CSGO-xxxxx codes can no longer be imported.

How to choose your crosshair

  • Color: pick one that contrasts with every map. Cyan, green and yellow are the most popular for a reason.
  • Size: a small crosshair is precise but can get lost in smokes and flashes; a slightly longer one is easier to find.
  • Gap: keep it small so the target's head stays visible between the lines.
  • Static: a crosshair that never moves (style 4) gives you a constant reference, which is why almost every pro uses it.

CS2 Crosshair Guide FAQ

How do I import a crosshair in CS2?

Copy the code, open Settings › Game › Crosshair, click Share or Import and paste it.

Why don't CSGO- crosshair codes work anymore?

Since the CS2 update of September 30, 2026, crosshair codes use a new format starting with CS, with sizes in pixels. Old CSGO- codes can no longer be imported.

What is the most popular crosshair among CS2 pros?

A small static crosshair: on ClutchCrosshair, most pros use a short length, a small gap and a cyan or green color, without a center dot.
