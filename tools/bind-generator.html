    How to use your CS2 binds

    1. Build your bind above, then click Copy.
    2. In CS2, open the console with ~ (enable it in Settings › Game if needed), paste and press Enter.
    3. To keep several binds safe, click Download autoexec.cfg, put the file in Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg and add +exec autoexec to your launch options.

    CS2 jump throw bind

    A jump throw bind makes you jump and release the grenade at the exact same moment, so your smokes and flashes land in the same place every time. Paste these three lines (change the key in the last line):

    alias "+jumpthrow" "+jump;-attack;-attack2"
alias "-jumpthrow" "-jump"
bind "v" "+jumpthrow"

    CS2 bind generator FAQ

    How do I make a bind in CS2?

    Open the console (~) and type bind followed by the key and the command between quotes, for example bind "kp_end" "buy ak47; buy m4a1". The ClutchCrosshair bind generator writes the line for you.

    Do buy binds still work in CS2?

    Yes. Buy commands such as buy ak47 or buy vesthelm work in CS2. They buy the weapon equipped in that slot of your loadout, so buy m4a1 gives you the M4A1-S if that is the one you equipped.

    How do I remove a bind in CS2?

    Type unbind followed by the key in the console, for example unbind kp_end.

    How do I save my CS2 binds?

    Binds you type in the console are saved automatically. To keep a full list safe, put your binds in an autoexec.cfg file in game/csgo/cfg and add +exec autoexec to your launch options. The generator can download that file for you.

    What is a jump throw bind?

    A bind that jumps and releases your grenade at the same moment, so you throw the same lineup every time. Hold the grenade with the attack button pressed, then press your jump throw key.
