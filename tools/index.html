CS2 Tools

Free tools to set up CS2 faster. Everything runs in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

The crosshair generator shows your crosshair at its real size on a CS2 map, exactly as it looks in game, even in 4:3 stretched. Start from the crosshair of a pro player like ZywOo or donk, change the length, gap, thickness and color, then copy the share code or the console commands.

The bind generator writes the console command for you: buy binds for a full buy in one key, a jump throw bind for repeatable smokes, a key to toggle the radar zoom or the volume. Keep all your binds in one list and download them as a ready-made autoexec.cfg.
